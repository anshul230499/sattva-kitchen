A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

What does sattvic mean here?

Sattva is associated with clarity, steadiness and balance in Indian philosophy. In cooking, traditions often emphasize freshness, simplicity and care.

These are vegetarian recipes made without onion or garlic. They're inspired by that approach, rather than a strict guide to every interpretation of sattvic food. Dairy, fermented foods and certain ingredients are viewed differently across traditions.
